# Conscious Compass Beta Tester Guide

Welcome to the Conscious Compass beta! Thank you for helping us refine this brand assessment tool.

## What is Conscious Compass?

Conscious Compass is a brand assessment framework that evaluates brands across 8 attributes of consciousness:

| Attribute | Question It Answers |
|-----------|---------------------|
| **Awake** | How well does the brand shape narratives and lead industry discourse? |
| **Aware** | Does the brand understand its audiences and build trust? |
| **Reflective** | Does the brand have authentic alignment between claims and reality? |
| **Attentive** | Does the brand deliver exceptional, consistent experiences? |
| **Cogent** | Is marketing driven by strategic insights and data? |
| **Sentient** | Does the brand create emotional connections that inspire action? |
| **Visionary** | Does the brand point toward something meaningful? |
| **Intentional** | Does the brand show up with substance, confidence, and leadership? |

## Running an Assessment

### Step 1: Setup
- Enter the brand name and website URL
- Select industry and business model (B2B, B2C, or Both)

### Step 2: Website Assessment
- **Auto-Assess**: Click to run AI analysis of the website
- **Pages Reviewed**: List pages you examined
- **Recognition & Credentials**: Note any awards, certifications, or speaking engagements visible
- **Screenshots**: Upload up to 4 screenshots of key pages
- **SEO Visibility**: Click "Assess SEO" for search visibility analysis
- **Technical Audit**: Open PageSpeed Insights and enter the scores manually

### Step 3: Social Media Assessment
Expand each accordion section and provide:
- **LinkedIn**: Company profile, recent posts with engagement
- **X (Twitter)**: Bio, recent tweets, engagement
- **Instagram**: Visual brand, content themes
- **Other Platforms**: YouTube, Reddit, Wikipedia presence
- **Influencer & Creator Partnerships**: Any sponsored content or ambassadors
- **Paid Media Presence**: Check ad libraries (Meta, Google, LinkedIn, TikTok)
- **Reputation**: Glassdoor, Nextdoor, WIPO trademark status

### Step 4: AI Reputation
Test how AI assistants perceive the brand:
- Open Claude, Gemini, and ChatGPT
- Ask: "What do you know about [Brand Name]?"
- Paste responses into each field

### Step 5: Earned Media
- Paste recent media coverage (last 3 months)
- Include: articles, podcasts, keynotes, awards, analyst mentions
- Click "Auto-Assess" for AI analysis

### Step 6: Report
- Review scores and recommendations
- Export as PDF, DOCX, or copy text
- Save to your account for future reference

## Tips for Better Assessments

### Be Thorough
The more evidence you provide, the more accurate the scoring. Include:
- Multiple social platforms
- Recent media coverage
- Technical performance scores
- AI reputation checks

### Use the Ad Libraries
These are goldmines for understanding brand investment:
- [Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library)
- [Google Ads Transparency](https://adstransparency.google.com)
- [LinkedIn Ad Library](https://www.linkedin.com/ad-library)
- [TikTok Ad Center](https://library.tiktok.com)

### Check Wikipedia
Wikipedia presence strongly impacts AI reputation and AWAKE scores.

### Don't Skip WIPO
Trademark registration signals professionalism and impacts INTENTIONAL scores.
- [WIPO Brand Database](https://branddb.wipo.int/en/similarname)

## FAQ

**Q: How long does an assessment take?**
A: A thorough assessment takes 20-30 minutes. Quick assessments can be done in 10-15 minutes.

**Q: Can I save partial progress?**
A: Yes, click "Save" at any point. Your work is also auto-saved periodically.

**Q: Can I reassess a brand later?**
A: Yes, load a saved assessment and click "Rescore" to regenerate with updated methodology.

**Q: Who can see my assessments?**
A: Only you can see your saved assessments. Admins can see the Compass Results summary.

**Q: What's the scoring scale?**
A: 0-100 for each attribute, with maturity stages:
- 0-39: Unconscious
- 40-54: Emerging
- 55-69: Developing
- 70-84: Conscious
- 85-100: Transcendent
